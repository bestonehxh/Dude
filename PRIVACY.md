# Dude — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Dude does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your labels stay on your Mac.** Rows and label settings are saved only in the app's own
  preferences on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Printer connection.** Dude talks to your Brother PT-P710BT over Bluetooth or USB only to read
  its status and send the labels you print.
- **Files.** Dude reads or writes a CSV file only when you choose one in Import or Export.
- **No analytics, tracking or advertising.**
- **Updates.** The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for a newer release. That request
  contains no information about you or your labels. The Mac App Store version does not do this;
  the App Store handles its updates.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bestonehxh/Dude/issues
